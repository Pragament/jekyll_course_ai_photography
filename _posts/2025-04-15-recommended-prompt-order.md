### Subject & Action

If your image has a subject, the prompt should be written to amplify its presence first and any actions the subject takes afterward.

First describe the image and then the things around it and how they are interacting with the surroundings. Also you can use layers to give more details like foreground and background details. Shadow details. How the subject is interacting with the environment and subject’s feeling and emotion. In the end you can also tell the overall vibe and mood of the scene.
