# Table Data: Camera Type Categories and Their Characteristics

As photographers, we often hear about different camera types, such as DSLR or mirrorless cameras, but have you ever stopped to think about the more specific categories within these broad groups? Cameras can be categorized based on their sensor size, resolution, and other features that make them suitable for particular uses. This table aims to provide an overview of the camera type categories, specific camera types, and their representative models, as well as key features and characteristics.

In this module, we will explore the first category: Medium Format Digital cameras. These cameras are designed for high-end professional applications where absolute image quality is paramount. The largest sensor size among all camera formats, medium format digital cameras offer exceptional image quality and detail, making them ideal for commercial photography, fashion photography, landscape photography, fine art photography, and advertising.

**Table: Medium Format Digital Cameras**

| **Camera Type Category** | **Specific Camera Type** | **Brand(s)** | **Representative Models** | **Key Features/Characteristics** | **Typical Uses** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium Format Digital | High-Resolution Pro | Hasselblad, Phase One, Fujifilm | Hasselblad X series (X1D, X2D), Hasselblad H series, Phase One XF system, Phase One XT system, Fujifilm GFX series (GFX100S, GFX50S II) | Features: Largest sensor size (significantly larger than Full-Frame), exceptional image quality and detail, very high resolution, shallow depth of field at a different scale, often modular systems, highest professional image quality. <br> Range: High-end professional, extremely expensive, for specialized professional applications. | Uses: High-end commercial photography, Fashion photography, Landscape photography (highest detail), Fine art photography, Advertising, Situations demanding the absolute highest image quality and resolution. |

As you can see from the table, medium format digital cameras are characterized by their large sensor size, which allows them to capture exceptional details and resolutions. The representative models listed include Hasselblad's X series and H series, Phase One's XF system and XT system, and Fujifilm's GFX series. These cameras are often used in high-end commercial photography, fashion photography, landscape photography, fine art photography, and advertising.

In conclusion, this table provides a comprehensive overview of medium format digital cameras, highlighting their key features and characteristics. Understanding these categories can help photographers choose the right camera for their specific needs and applications, ensuring they capture stunning images that meet their creative vision.
