# Table Data: Camera Type Categories

Camera technology has evolved significantly over the years, offering a wide range of options for capturing high-quality images. In this module, we'll delve into the world of camera type categories, exploring the various types of cameras and their characteristics.

One of the most significant categorizations in the camera industry is the distinction between camcorders and other types of cameras. Camcorders are designed primarily for video recording, offering a range of features that cater to the specific needs of videographers. These cameras typically feature long zoom lenses, comfortable ergonomics for handheld video shooting, built-in microphones, and audio controls.
