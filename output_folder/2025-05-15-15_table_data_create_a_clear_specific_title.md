As we delve into the world of lighting in photography and filmmaking, it's essential to understand the various types of light sources available. In this module, we'll explore the differences between natural and artificial lighting, as well as their characteristics, uses, and effects.

Natural Light
------------

The first type of lighting we'll examine is natural light, which originates from the sun, moon, and stars. Natural light can be divided into several subcategories, including:
